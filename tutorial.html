Build your marketing agent.

Five steps, no code. The agent drafts. You decide.

Prof. Francesca Sotgiu · LUISS Business School Amsterdam1
What it is

An agent is a model with a brief.

1Instructions

Who the agent is, what it does, how it writes, and what it must never do.

2Knowledge

Files it can consult: your brief, positioning map, product facts.

3A name

So you can reuse it, and every chat starts from the same brief.

Build your marketing agent3
Step 1 · Write the brief

Your brief is your STP, written down.

  • Role: who the agent is and what it helps with
  • Positioning: your positioning statement and target
  • Tone of voice: three adjectives, plus words to avoid
  • Facts: only what it may state, with sources
  • Red lines: claims, prices or topics it must never touch
  • Format: length, language, structure of the answer
Build your marketing agent4
Example · Maison Arno (fictional)

An example brief.

You are the marketing assistant of Maison Arno, a Florentine leather goods house.
POSITIONING  For clients who value quiet craftsmanship, Maison Arno makes bags designed
             and assembled in Florence that last a lifetime.
TONE         Calm, precise, warm. Never use: "must-have", "deal", "hurry".
FACTS        Use only the attached fact sheet.
             Leather: certified tanneries in Tuscany and Spain.
RED LINES    Never offer discounts on the Arno Tote or other icons.
             Never write generic claims such as "sustainable" or "eco-friendly".
             If you are unsure of a fact, say so and ask. Do not invent.
FORMAT       English, under 80 words, end with one question for the reader.
Build your marketing agent5
Step 2 · Build it

Five minutes of set-up.

  1. Create a new Gem, Project or GPT.
  2. Paste your brief into the instructions field.
  3. Upload knowledge files: brief, positioning map, fact sheet. No personal or confidential data.
  4. Give it a name, for example "Arno Marketing Assistant".
  5. Test it: "Describe our brand in two sentences." Does it sound like your positioning?

Step 3 · Give it three tasks

Three tasks. Save every draft.

Launch post

"Write an Instagram post for the launch of our new collection."

Customer reply

"A client's strap broke after two months. Write our reply."

Promotion

"Propose a promotion to raise sales next quarter."

Step 4 · Be the human in the loop

Check every draft on five points.

CheckAsk
TruthIs every fact in the brief or a cited source?
ClaimsIs any environmental or origin claim specific and verifiable?
Brand fitDoes it reflect the positioning, at the level of values?
ValueDoes it protect price positioning and customer lifetime value?
ContextDoes it work for this market, culture and channel?

Step 5 · Report

One slide. Full transparency.

In your presentation
