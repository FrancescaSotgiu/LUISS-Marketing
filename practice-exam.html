The case

Read it once fully. You can come back to it at any time with the "Case" button.

Maison Arno is a Florentine leather goods house founded in 1962. Its bags are designed and assembled by hand in its Florence atelier, from leather tanned by certified partners in Tuscany and Spain. Revenue is €180 million, 70% of it in Europe. Its two icons are the Arno Tote (€2,400) and the Ponte clutch (€1,600). The brand has a simple rule: it never discounts its icons.

The board has budget for one new flagship store in 2027. Two options are on the table: Tokyo (Japan) and Dubai (United Arab Emirates). The Dubai team is enthusiastic and proposes a launch weekend with 20% off the icons "to build a client base fast". The Tokyo team warns that Japanese clients are demanding and loyal, but slow to try new brands.

At the same time, Maison Arno is launching Arno Rinasce ("Arno reborn"), a capsule collection made partly from leather offcuts from its own atelier. The capsule will launch in Europe and in the new flagship. The marketing team has drafted five claims for it (Exhibit 3).

You are the new Marketing Manager International. The CEO wants your recommendation.

Exhibit 3 · Draft claims for Arno Rinasce

  1. "Our most sustainable collection ever: eco-friendly luxury."
  2. "Made with 60% leather offcuts from our Florence atelier that would otherwise be discarded. Share verified by an independent auditor."
  3. "A carbon neutral bag: we offset all its emissions by planting trees in Tuscany."
  4. "We repair every Arno Rinasce bag for free in our Florence atelier for ten years."
  5. "Net zero by 2030."
